Know the price of every call.

A free pot odds and equity calculator for Texas Hold'em that also reviews your PokerStars sessions. Enter the pot and the bet, and it tells you instantly whether calling pays.

Windows · Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 and later) · No account, no internet connection.

  1. The calculator

    A verdict, not a formula.

    Type the pot and what it costs to call, then click your cards in the deck. Breakeven shows the pot odds and the equity you need, then runs 5,000 simulated hands to find yours.

    • A clear verdict

      ✓ CALL or ✕ FOLD, with your equity, the equity you need and the EV of calling.

    • Outs and danger cards

      The cards that put you ahead turn green, and the ones that help them turn red.

    • No button to press

      It updates as you type and runs 5,000 simulated hands.

  2. Session review

    Every spot you faced a bet, graded.

    Breakeven picks up your PokerStars hand histories on its own and checks the price you paid: calls that paid, calls that didn't, and folds worth a second look.

    Know who you're up against. See how loose, tight, passive or aggressive everyone at your table plays, with what that usually means for you.

  3. Private and free

  4. Questions

    The things players ask first.

    Is it allowed on PokerStars?

    Breakeven only reads finished hands from the files PokerStars writes itself. It never reads the PokerStars window or memory, and never looks at a hand in progress. Poker sites ban software that advises you during a hand, and trackers built on your own hand histories are a long-accepted category. Breakeven isn't on PokerStars' list of approved tools, though, so check their current rules before keeping it open beside a real-money table. If in doubt, review after you play. More on playing by the rules.

    Does it send my hands anywhere?

    No. Breakeven makes no network connections at all. Your hands stay on your computer.

    Does it account for implied odds or reads?

    No. The verdict is pot odds against random hands. That's the honest baseline. Your read on the other players is still yours to add.

    Which games does it support?

    Texas Hold'em, in cash games and tournaments. Hand history review reads PokerStars hands. Earlier releases were called Poker Helper.

A warning the first time you open it?

That's expected. The app isn't signed yet (signing costs money every year), so the first launch shows a warning. You only have to do this once.

  • Windows shows "Windows protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway.
  • Mac says the app "can't be opened". Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway. On older macOS versions, right-click the app and choose Open instead.
